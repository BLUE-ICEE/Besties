RABBIT HOLES

Things that are oddly specific.

The kind of facts that make you stop and open another tab.

01 Bananas are berries.

Botanically, berries develop from one flower with one ovary. Strawberries don't fit that definition.

02 Saturn could float.

Its average density is lower than water. You'd just need a bathtub on an absurd scale.

03 Octopuses have three hearts.

Two pump blood toward the gills and one pumps it around the body.

04 Honey lasts unusually long.

Its low water content and acidity make it unusually resistant to microbial growth when stored properly.

05 A Venus day is longer than its year.

Venus rotates so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one trip around the Sun.

06 Wombat poop is cube-shaped.

The unusual shape forms inside the intestine.

07 Some turtles can breathe through their skin.

Several species can absorb oxygen through specialized surfaces while underwater.

08 There are clouds in space.

Interstellar clouds are huge regions of gas and dust where stars can eventually form.

09 Sharks are older than trees.

Shark ancestors appear in the fossil record before modern forests evolved.

10 Some fungi glow.

Bioluminescent fungi produce light through chemical reactions.

11 Water can boil and freeze at once.

At its triple point, water can exist as solid, liquid and gas simultaneously.

12 Space phenomena can become sound.

Scientists can translate measurements of plasma and other space phenomena into audio.
